ShopKart Refund Policy
Version 3.2 - applies to all orders placed on ShopKart.

1. Overview
We want you to be happy with every order. If something is not right, you can ask for a refund through our support chat. This policy explains which orders can be refunded, how long you have, and how the money comes back to you.

2. Return window
You can request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days start on the day the order is marked as delivered, not the day you placed it. Requests made after 30 days cannot be refunded, but you may still be able to get a repair under the product's warranty.

3. Items that cannot be refunded
Gift cards and e-vouchers cannot be refunded once they are delivered, because they can be used immediately. Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be refunded either; you can cancel them from the Orders page instead.

4. How much is refunded
For apparel and electronics we refund 100 percent of the amount you paid for the item. If part of the order was already refunded, only the remaining amount can be refunded. Delivery charges are refunded only if the whole order is returned.

5. Large refunds need a human review
Any refund above 2,000 rupees is reviewed by our support team before it is approved. The chat assistant will create a review ticket for you. A team member will contact you within 24 hours.

6. How you receive the money
Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. UPI and wallet refunds arrive within 2 business days. Card refunds can take 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

7. Damaged or wrong items
If you received a damaged or wrong item, tell us within 48 hours of delivery and share a photo in the chat. These cases are always sent to our team, whatever the amount.

8. Contact
You can reach the ShopKart support team in the app chat, every day from 8 AM to 10 PM.
